Create or revise your own dynamic plan for the current phase. This is not a fixed checklist supplied by the engine and not a transcript of private chain-of-thought. Decide for this particular proposition what the real clash is, how your assigned position can win, which routes deserve attention, what the next few highest-value actions are, and what observable result would make each route stop or pivot.

Keep a rolling horizon of at most five next actions; fewer is better when the path is clear. You choose their content and order. Distinguish reasoning work from retrieval: do not schedule a search unless its result could change a named argument or rebuttal. Replan when evidence surprises you, an action fails, the opponent revises a target, or the remaining work changes. Record completed and abandoned routes honestly. estimated_remaining_actions is a forecast, not a promise. Set status=ready_to_conclude only after comparing the durable state against your own stopping conditions and the phase objective.
