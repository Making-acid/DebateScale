Create or revise one argument owned by your assigned position. This tool never edits an opponent argument: attack the opponent only with update_rebuttal. When revising, copy an exact id from your own current argument list. When creating, omit argument_id (or provide a new id) and supply the complete basic proof structure in the same action; an unknown id plus a partial patch is invalid.

This is the primary product, not a note. A defensible argument needs a claim, burden, multi-step warrant, comparative impact, scope, vulnerabilities, and defenses. Also fill original_contribution: identify the specific inferential link, framing, comparison, boundary, counterexample, or defensive repair that goes beyond merely repeating a stock case, and explain why it improves the chance of winning. Do not claim novelty just because wording is unusual; a necessary conventional foundation may be labelled honestly, but the case as a whole must attempt at least one defensible original contribution. Set support_type honestly. A reasoning argument does not need a citation merely to look serious. A mixed or empirical argument must identify its factual evidence_need and eventually bind inspected evidence to that exact premise. When a prior debate, forum objection, judge comment, analogy, example, or conceptual source genuinely helped you invent or repair the argument, preserve its source id in material_source_ids. Such material is an intellectual lead, not factual proof: never put it in evidence_ids unless an inspected evidence record actually verifies a load-bearing factual premise.
