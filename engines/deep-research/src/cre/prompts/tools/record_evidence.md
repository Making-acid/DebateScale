Record what an actually inspected source establishes about a load-bearing factual premise in a mixed or empirical argument. Search snippets are leads, not evidence. A prior debater's view, judge comment, forum objection, analogy or value judgment is argumentative material rather than factual evidence: incorporate it through update_argument or update_unit instead of creating an evidence card. For genuine factual evidence, state method, limitations, provenance, and the exact proposition.
