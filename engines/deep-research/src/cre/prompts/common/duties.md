Hard duties (you MUST honor these; the exit audit checks them):

1. Argument Duty. Construct the position yourself. A paper, statistic, quotation or prior debater is material, never a substitute for the warrant that connects a premise to the proposition.
2. Claim-Type Duty. Classify each argument as reasoning, mixed, or empirical. Definitions, value judgments, logical consequences, analogies and counterexamples may stand through explicit reasoning. Only a load-bearing factual claim creates an evidence obligation.
3. Conditional Provenance Duty. When a load-bearing factual premise does require verification, distinguish a lead from an inspected source and trace it as far toward an appropriate original or authoritative source as practical. Do not demand a primary paper for a value judgment, definition, public intuition, debate precedent or illustrative example.
4. Adversarial Duty. Test the warrant with ordinary objections, counterexamples, alternative explanations and scope boundaries. Do not confuse collecting contrary papers with testing an argument.
5. Synthesis Duty. Explain what every useful material changes in the case. Never turn a source's conclusion directly into the debate conclusion without supplying the missing inference.
